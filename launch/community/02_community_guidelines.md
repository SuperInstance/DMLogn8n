# DMLog Community Guidelines

## **Our Community Vision**

Welcome to the DMLog community! We're building a space where Dungeon Masters, developers, AI researchers, and D&D enthusiasts can collaborate to create the next generation of intelligent gaming characters. Our community is founded on respect, learning, and creative exploration.

---

## **🎯 Core Values**

### **1. Learning and Growth**
- We believe characters (and people) should improve through experience
- Share knowledge generously and learn from others
- Embrace constructive feedback as a tool for growth
- Celebrate both successes and learning opportunities

### **2. Respect and Inclusion**
- Welcome community members of all experience levels
- Respect different perspectives and approaches to gaming
- Create an environment where everyone feels valued
- Practice patience with newcomers and questions

### **3. Creativity and Innovation**
- Encourage creative uses of DMLog technology
- Share innovative character concepts and campaign ideas
- Explore the boundaries of AI-assisted storytelling
- Think beyond traditional gaming paradigms

### **4. Transparency and Trust**
- Be honest about your experiences with DMLog
- Share both successes and challenges openly
- Respect privacy and confidentiality
- Build trust through consistent, reliable participation

---

## **📋 Community Rules**

### **Behavior Guidelines**

#### **✅ Do:**
- **Be Constructive**: Help others solve problems and improve their experience
- **Share Knowledge**: Contribute what you've learned about DMLog and D&D
- **Stay On-Topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the channel's purpose
- **Use Appropriate Channels**: Post in the right place for maximum visibility
- **Celebrate Success**: Share your achievements and celebrate others' successes
- **Ask Questions**: No question is too basic if you've tried to find the answer first
- **Respect Privacy**: Don't share personal information about others without consent
- **Credit Sources**: Give credit when using others' ideas or code

#### **❌ Don't:**
- **Spam or Self-Promote**: No excessive self-promotion or irrelevant links
- **Harass or Bully**: No personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination
- **Share Inappropriate Content**: No NSFW, offensive, or illegal content
- **Violate Privacy**: Don't share others' private conversations or data
- **Circumvent Rules**: Don't try to find loopholes in community guidelines
- **Create Drama**: Don't engage in public arguments or drama
- **Spoil Stories**: Use spoiler tags for campaign/story content
- **Mislead Others**: Don't spread false information about DMLog

### **Content Guidelines**

#### **Technical Content**
- Share code snippets with proper formatting
- Include error messages and context when asking for help
- Use appropriate channels for technical discussions
- Respect intellectual property and licensing

#### **Character and Campaign Content**
- Share character stories respectfully
- Use spoiler tags for plot-sensitive content
- Get permission before sharing others' campaign details
- Be mindful of copyrighted material

#### **Research Content**
- Follow academic integrity guidelines
- Properly cite sources and references
- Respect participant privacy in research
- Follow IRB guidelines for human subjects

## **🔧 Support and Help**

### **Getting Help Effectively**

#### **Before Asking:**
1. **Search First**: Look for existing answers in channel history
2. **Check Documentation**: Review relevant docs at docs.dmlog.ai
3. **Check GitHub Issues**: Look for reported problems
4. **Try Basic Troubleshooting**: Restart, update, check configuration

#### **When Asking for Help:**
```
Include this information:
1. DMLog version
2. Operating system and specs
3. What you're trying to accomplish
4. What you've already tried
5. Error messages (full text)
6. Screenshots if relevant
```

#### **Where to Ask:**
- **General Questions**: #general-chat
- **Technical Issues**: #technical-support
- **Bug Reports**: #bug-reports (with template)
- **Feature Requests**: #ideas-and-suggestions
- **API Questions**: #api-integration
- **Research Questions**: #research-and-academia

### **Providing Help**

#### **Guidelines for Helpers:**
- **Be Patient**: Remember everyone was a beginner once
- **Ask Questions**: Understand the problem before offering solutions
- **Provide Context**: Explain why your solution works
- **Encourage Learning**: Help others understand, not just copy-paste
- **Know Your Limits**: Don't be afraid to say "I don't know"
- **Escalate When Needed**: Tag moderators or developers for complex issues

## **🚨 Conflict Resolution**

### **Disagreement Guidelines**

#### **When You Disagree:**
1. **Stay Calm**: Take a break if emotions are high
2. **Be Specific**: Address the issue, not the person
3. **Listen First**: Understand the other perspective
4. **Find Common Ground**: Look for shared interests
5. **Agree to Disagree**: It's okay to have different opinions
6. **Ask for Help**: Involve moderators if needed

#### **When Others Disagree:**
1. **Don't Engage**: Avoid escalating conflicts
2. **Report Issues**: Use appropriate channels to report problems
3. **Provide Context**: Help moderators understand the situation
4. **Move On**: Don't dwell on resolved issues

### **Reporting Problems**

#### **What to Report:**
- Harassment or bullying
- Spam or inappropriate content
- Security vulnerabilities
- Privacy violations
- Severe rule violations

#### **How to Report:**
1. **Message Moderators**: Send a DM to @moderators
2. **Use Report Command**: Type !report [user] [reason]
3. **Provide Evidence**: Include screenshots or context
4. **Be Specific**: Clearly explain the issue
5. **Follow Up**: Respond to moderator questions

#### **Response Timeline:**
- **Urgent Issues**: 30 minutes
- **Standard Reports**: 4 hours
- **Complex Issues**: 24 hours
- **Formal Appeals**: 48-72 hours
