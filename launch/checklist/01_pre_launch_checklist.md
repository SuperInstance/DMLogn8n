## **🆘 Emergency Procedures**

### **Critical Issues**
- **System Down**: Immediate team mobilization, status page update
- **Security Breach**: Security team activation, user notification
- **Data Loss**: Recovery procedures activated, incident investigation
- **Performance Issues**: Scaling adjustments, optimization efforts

### **Communication Protocols**
- **Internal**: Team communication channel, designated coordinator
- **External**: Status page updates, social media announcements
- **Support**: Tiered escalation, clear communication templates
- **Media**: Designated spokesperson, prepared statements
