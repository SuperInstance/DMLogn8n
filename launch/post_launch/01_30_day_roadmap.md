# DMLog 30-Day Post-Launch Roadmap

## **Executive Summary**

This 30-day roadmap outlines the strategic priorities, feature developments, and community initiatives for DMLog following launch. The focus is on user onboarding, system stabilization, feedback collection, and rapid iteration based on real-world usage.

---

## **🎯 Post-Launch Strategic Objectives**

### **Primary Goals**
1. **User Success**: Ensure 80%+ of new users successfully onboard and create their first learning character
2. **System Stability**: Maintain 99.9% uptime and <50ms response times under growing load
3. **Community Growth**: Build an active community of 2,000+ engaged users
4. **Feature Validation**: Gather data on feature usage to prioritize development roadmap
5. **Market Validation**: Achieve 1,000+ active users and validate product-market fit

### **Key Success Metrics**
- **User Metrics**: 1,000+ active users, 80% onboarding completion, 70% 7-day retention
- **Technical Metrics**: 99.9% uptime, <50ms p95 response time, <2% error rate
- **Community Metrics**: 2,000+ Discord members, 500+ daily active community members
- **Business Metrics**: 200+ paying customers, 20% conversion rate from free trial
