## Brand Guidelines

### Brand Identity

#### Logo Usage

**Primary Logo**
```
[DMLOG]
Dungeons & Dragons Campaign Management
```

**Logo Clear Space**
- Minimum clear space: 1x the height of the logo
- No text or graphics should infringe on this space

**Logo Variations**
- **Horizontal**: Primary version for most applications
- **Stacked**: Vertical version for limited spaces
- **Monochrome**: Single color for specific applications
- **Icon**: D20 die icon for app icons and favicons

#### Color Palette

**Primary Colors**
```
DMLog Blue: #1E3A8A (Dark Blue)
DMLog Light: #3B82F6 (Light Blue)
DMLog Accent: #F59E0B (Amber)
```

**Secondary Colors**
```
Success: #10B981 (Green)
Warning: #F59E0B (Amber)
Error: #EF4444 (Red)
Neutral: #6B7280 (Gray)
```

**Usage Guidelines**
- Primary blue should dominate the brand identity
- Accent color for calls-to-action and highlights
- Secondary colors for status indicators and specific use cases
- Maintain contrast ratios for accessibility (WCAG AA compliance)

#### Typography

**Primary Font Families**
- **Headings**: Inter (Bold, 600-700)
- **Body Text**: Inter (Regular, 400)
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono
- **Fantasy**: Cinzel (for D&D-themed headers)

**Typography Hierarchy**
```
H1: 2.5rem (40px) - Inter Bold
H2: 2rem (32px) - Inter SemiBold
H3: 1.5rem (24px) - Inter SemiBold
H4: 1.25rem (20px) - Inter Medium
Body: 1rem (16px) - Inter Regular
Small: 0.875rem (14px) - Inter Regular
```

#### Voice and Tone

**Brand Personality**
- **Knowledgeable**: Deep understanding of D&D and gaming
- **Approachable**: Friendly and welcoming to all skill levels
- **Innovative**: Modern technology with respect for tradition
- **Reliable**: Dependable and professional service

**Communication Style**
- **Clear and Concise**: Avoid jargon, explain complex concepts simply
- **Enthusiastic**: Show passion for D&D and gaming
- **Helpful**: Focus on solving user problems
- **Inclusive**: Welcome all types of players and DMs

## Analytics and Measurement

### Key Performance Indicators

#### User Acquisition Metrics
- **New User Signups**: Daily/weekly/monthly registration rates
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Marketing spend per new user
- **Conversion Rate**: Sign-up to paid conversion rates
- **Channel Performance**: Effectiveness by acquisition channel

#### Engagement Metrics
- **Daily Active Users (DAU)**: Daily engagement rates
- **Session Duration**: Time spent in platform
- **Feature Adoption**: Usage rates for key features
- **User Retention**: 1, 7, 30-day retention rates

#### Business Metrics
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Monthly subscription revenue
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Long-term customer value
- **Churn Rate**: Customer cancellation rates
- **Expansion Revenue**: Upgrade and add-on revenue

### Marketing Analytics

#### Campaign Performance
- **Click-Through Rates (CTR)**: Email and ad performance
- **Conversion Rates**: Campaign to conversion metrics
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**: Advertising effectiveness
- **Brand Awareness**: Social media reach and engagement

#### Content Performance
- **Blog Traffic**: Website visitors and engagement
- **Video Views**: YouTube and video platform metrics
- **Social Media Engagement**: Likes, shares, comments
- **Lead Generation**: Content marketing conversions

### User Analytics

#### Behavior Analysis
- **Feature Usage**: Which features are most popular
- **User Journey**: Common paths through the application
- **Drop-off Points**: Where users struggle or leave
- **Power User Identification**: Most engaged users

#### Feedback Analysis
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Customer satisfaction
- **Customer Support Tickets**: Common issues and concerns
- **Feature Requests**: User-suggested improvements
- **Qualitative Feedback**: User comments and reviews

### Reporting Dashboard

#### Executive Dashboard
- **Key Metrics Overview**: Critical KPIs at a glance
- **Revenue Tracking**: Financial performance
- **User Growth**: Acquisition and retention trends
- **Competitive Analysis**: Market position and trends

#### Marketing Dashboard
- **Campaign Performance**: Real-time campaign metrics
- **Channel Analytics**: Performance by marketing channel
- **Content Engagement**: Content marketing effectiveness
- **Lead Generation**: Marketing pipeline metrics

#### Product Dashboard
- **User Engagement**: Feature usage and adoption
- **Performance Metrics**: System performance and reliability
- **User Feedback**: Customer satisfaction and issues
- **Development Progress**: Feature development and releases
