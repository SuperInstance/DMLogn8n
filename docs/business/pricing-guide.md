## Pricing Philosophy

DMLog is committed to providing accessible pricing that delivers exceptional value while supporting sustainable development. Our pricing strategy is based on these core principles:

### Value-Based Pricing
- **Fair Value**: Each tier provides clear value proportional to its cost
- **Feature Alignment**: Pricing matches the sophistication and utility of features
- **ROI Focus**: Help users understand the return on their investment

### Accessibility First
- **Free Forever**: Core functionality remains free for all users
- **Gradual Upsell**: Users can upgrade as their needs grow
- **No Hidden Costs**: Transparent pricing with no surprise charges

### Community Support
- **Educational Discounts**: Special pricing for schools and educational institutions
- **Creator Programs**: Support for content creators and streamers
- **Non-Profit Rates**: Discounted pricing for qualifying organizations

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Pricing Questions

**Q: Can I change my plan at any time?**
A: Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges, while downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

**Q: What happens to my data if I downgrade?**
A: Your data is never deleted. If you exceed the limits of a lower tier, you'll still have access to your existing data but won't be able to create new content until you're within the limits.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for annual billing?**
A: Yes! Annual billing saves you 17% compared to monthly billing - that's like getting 2 months free.

**Q: Is there a free trial for paid plans?**
A: We offer a 14-day free trial for Premium and Professional tiers. No credit card required to start your trial.

### Feature Questions

**Q: Can I use DMLog without an internet connection?**
A: Yes! Our mobile apps support offline mode for viewing character sheets and accessing downloaded content. You'll need to sync when you're back online.

**Q: What happens to my campaigns if I cancel my subscription?**
A: Your campaigns remain accessible but with free tier limitations. You can still access and export your data at any time.

**Q: Can I share my subscription with others?**
A: Each subscription is for individual use. However, our Professional and Enterprise tiers support multiple users under one account.

### Technical Questions

**Q: How secure is my payment information?**
A: We use industry-standard encryption and never store your payment information on our servers. All payments are processed through secure PCI-compliant payment processors.

**Q: Can I export my data if I decide to leave DMLog?**
A: Absolutely! You can export all your characters, campaigns, and data at any time in standard formats like JSON, PDF, and CSV.

**Q: Do you offer custom integrations?**
A: Enterprise customers receive full API access and custom integration support. Professional tier customers have access to basic API functionality.

### Business Questions

**Q: Do you offer educational pricing?**
A: Yes! We offer significant discounts for educational institutions, including free expanded tiers for K-12 schools and discounted plans for higher education.

**Q: Can I use DMLog for commercial D&D services?**
A: Professional and Enterprise tiers include commercial use licenses for running paid D&D games and services.

**Q: Do you have affiliate programs?**
A: Yes! We offer affiliate programs for content creators, game stores, and educational partners. Contact partners@dmlog.com for more information.
