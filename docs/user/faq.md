## Getting Started

### Q: What is DMLog?
**A:** DMLog is a comprehensive Dungeons & Dragons campaign management system that combines character management, campaign organization, real-time sessions, and AI-powered features in one platform. It's designed for both Dungeon Masters and players to enhance their D&D experience.

### Q: Is DMLog free to use?
**A:** DMLog offers a free tier with essential features for character management, basic campaign tools, and session participation. Premium features like AI personalities, advanced analytics, and unlimited campaigns require a subscription.

### Q: What D&D editions does DMLog support?
**A:** DMLog primarily supports D&D 5th Edition with full rules integration. We also provide basic support for other editions (Pathfinder, 3.5e, 4e) through custom character sheets and homebrew tools.

### Q: Do I need to install any software?
**A:** No! DMLog is a web-based application that works in any modern browser. We also offer mobile apps for iOS and Android for gaming on the go.

### Q: Can I use DMLog with existing characters?
**A:** Yes! You can manually create existing characters, or import them from D&D Beyond using our integration tools. You can also upload character sheets in PDF format as reference.

## Character Management

### Q: How do I create a character?
**A:**
1. Click "Characters" in the main navigation
2. Click "Create Character"
3. Fill in basic information (name, race, class, level)
4. Set ability scores (Standard Array, Point Buy, or Roll)
5. Select skills and proficiencies
6. Choose equipment and spells
7. Add personality traits and backstory
8. Save your character

### Q: Can I import characters from D&D Beyond?
**A:** Yes! DMLog integrates with D&D Beyond. Click "Import Character" and connect your D&D Beyond account to transfer characters automatically.

### Q: How does leveling up work?
**A:** When you gain enough XP, DMLog notifies you to level up. The process includes:
- Increasing hit points (roll or take average)
- Adding proficiency bonus
- Choosing new class features
- Selecting feats or ability score improvements
- Updating spell slots and abilities

### Q: Can I multi-class my character?
**A:** Absolutely! Click "Add Class" on your character sheet to multi-class. DMLog automatically calculates:
- Hit point gains from new class
- Proficiency bonus changes
- New abilities and features
- Spell casting progression

### Q: How do I manage equipment and inventory?
**A:** The equipment tab allows you to:
- Add/remove items with weight tracking
- Organize items into categories
- Track consumable usage
- Manage magic items and attunement
- Monitor carrying capacity

### Q: Can I customize my character sheet?
**A:** Yes! Premium users can:
- Upload custom character portraits
- Choose from different sheet themes
- Add custom fields and sections
- Create custom equipment and items

### Q: What happens if I accidentally delete my character?
**A:** Deleted characters are moved to trash for 30 days before permanent deletion. You can restore them during this time. Premium users have extended recovery options.

### Q: Can I share my character with others?
**A:** Yes! You can share your character sheet with:
- Your DM for easy reference
- Other players in your campaign
- Public sharing (social media, forums)
- Export as PDF or JSON

## Campaign Management

### Q: How do I create a campaign?
**A:**
1. Go to "Campaigns" and click "Create Campaign"
2. Enter campaign details (name, setting, difficulty)
3. Set player limits and scheduling preferences
4. Add campaign description and hooks
5. Invite players via email
6. Configure campaign settings and rules

### Q: Can I run multiple campaigns?
**A:** Free users can run 1 campaign at a time. Premium users can run unlimited campaigns with different groups and settings.

### Q: How do I invite players to my campaign?
**A:**
1. Go to your campaign dashboard
2. Click "Invite Players"
3. Enter player email addresses
4. Set their roles (Player, Co-DM, Observer)
5. Players receive email invitations to join

### Q: What's the difference between Player, Co-DM, and Observer roles?
**A:**
- **Player**: Full participation in sessions, character management
- **Co-DM**: Assist with campaign management, limited DM permissions
- **Observer**: View-only access to sessions and campaign content

### Q: Can I make my campaign public?
**A:** Yes! You can set your campaign to "Public" to allow new players to discover and request to join. Private campaigns are invite-only.

### Q: How do I manage campaign world-building?
**A:** DMLog provides tools for:
- Creating locations and maps
- Managing NPCs and factions
- Building timelines and histories
- Storing lore and documentation
- Organizing campaign assets

### Q: Can I use homebrew content?
**A:** Yes! DMLog supports homebrew content through:
- Custom character creation options
- Homebrew rules and mechanics
- Custom items and equipment
- Unique monsters and creatures
- Custom spells and abilities

## Sessions and Gameplay

### Q: How do I join a session?
**A:**
1. Receive session invitation (email or notification)
2. Click "Join Session" at scheduled time
3. Select your character for the session
4. Wait in lobby for DM to start
5. Participate using the session interface

### Q: What do I need for a session?
**A:** You'll need:
- Stable internet connection
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Microphone (for voice chat, optional)
- Webcam (for video chat, optional)

### Q: Can I use voice and video chat?
**A:** Yes! DMLog includes built-in voice chat using WebRTC technology. You can also integrate with Discord for voice communication.

### Q: How does the session recording work?
**A:** DMLog automatically records:
- Dice rolls and outcomes
- Chat messages and dialogue
- Combat events and turn order
- Character actions and decisions
- DM descriptions and narration

You can review recordings as text transcripts or replay sessions.

### Q: What if I'm late to a session?
**A:** You can join sessions at any time. The system will catch you up on what's happened so far through the session log and DM notes.

### Q: Can I spectate sessions?
**A:** Yes! With Observer role permission, you can watch sessions without actively participating. Perfect for learning or when you can't make a game.

### Q: How are experience points handled?
**A:** DMs can award XP through:
- Automatic combat XP calculations
- Session completion bonuses
- Roleplaying and story awards
- Milestone achievements
- Custom XP distribution

## Dice Rolling and Combat

### Q: How does the dice roller work?
**A:** The integrated dice roller features:
- All standard D&D dice (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100)
- Custom modifiers and calculations
- Advantage/disadvantage rolling
- Critical hit thresholds
- 3D animations and sound effects

### Q: Can I roll dice outside of sessions?
**A:** Yes! The dice roller is available anytime through the quick actions bar or character sheet.

### Q: How does combat tracking work?
**A:** DMLog's combat tracker includes:
- Automatic initiative rolling
- Turn order management
- Condition tracking with durations
- HP and damage tracking
- Status effects and bonuses
- Combat analytics and summary

### Q: Can I customize the dice roller?
**A:** Premium users can:
- Choose custom dice skins
- Set preferred animations
- Configure sound effects
- Create custom dice formulas
- Save frequently used rolls

### Q: How are critical hits handled?
**A:** DMLog automatically:
- Detects critical hits (natural 20 or custom threshold)
- Calculates critical damage (double dice + modifiers)
- Applies critical effects and conditions
- Tracks critical hit statistics

### Q: Can I use the dice roller for other tabletop games?
**A:** Yes! While optimized for D&D, the dice roller supports custom dice configurations for other tabletop RPGs.

## AI Features

### Q: What AI features does DMLog offer?
**A:** DMLog includes:
- Character personality modeling
- Decision-making assistance
- Memory formation and consolidation
- Cultural transmission between characters
- Story and encounter suggestions
- Character development insights

### Q: How does the character personality AI work?
**A:** The AI system:
- Analyzes your character's traits and background
- Models personality using the Big Five framework
- Suggests actions based on personality
- Evolves personality based on experiences
- Provides decision confidence scores

### Q: What are character memories?
**A:** The memory system:
- Automatically captures significant events
- Scores memories by importance
- Consolidates important memories over time
- Influences future decisions
- Creates character development arcs

### Q: Is the AI mandatory?
**A:** No! All AI features are optional. You can use DMLog's core features without enabling any AI functionality.

### Q: Does the AI make decisions for me?
**A:** The AI provides suggestions and insights, but you always make final decisions. It's designed to enhance, not replace, human creativity.

### Q: How does cultural transmission work?
**A:** Characters can:
- Learn from each other's experiences
- Share knowledge and skills
- Influence each other's personalities
- Develop cultural relationships over time

### Q: Is my character data used to train AI models?
**A:** No. Your character data is private and only used to provide features within your campaign. We don't use personal data for training external AI models.

## Technical Issues

### Q: My session is lagging or slow. What can I do?
**A:** Try these troubleshooting steps:
1. Check your internet connection speed
2. Close unnecessary browser tabs
3. Clear browser cache and cookies
4. Restart your browser
5. Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
6. Disable browser extensions

### Q: I can't connect to a session. What should I check?
**A:** Verify the following:
- You have a stable internet connection
- Your browser allows WebRTC connections
- Firewall isn't blocking DMLog
- Session invitation is still valid
- You're using a supported browser

### Q: The dice roller isn't working. How do I fix it?
**A:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Check JavaScript is enabled
3. Clear browser cache
4. Disable ad blockers temporarily
5. Try incognito/private browsing mode

### Q: My character sheet won't save changes. What's wrong?
**A:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify you're logged in
3. Look for error messages
4. Try saving smaller sections individually
5. Contact support if issues persist

### Q: How do I report bugs or issues?
**A:**
1. Use the in-app feedback button
2. Email support@dmlog.com
3. Report on our GitHub issues page
4. Join our Discord community
5. Include screenshots and details when possible

### Q: Is my data backed up?
**A:** Yes! We backup all data daily with:
- Automated daily backups
- Point-in-time recovery options
- Redundant storage systems
- Data export capabilities

## Privacy and Security

### Q: How is my data protected?
**A:** We implement multiple security measures:
- 256-bit SSL encryption for all data
- Secure password hashing
- Regular security audits
- GDPR and CCPA compliance
- Limited employee data access

### Q: Who can see my characters and campaigns?
**A:** Privacy settings control visibility:
- Private: Only you and invited players
- Public: Discoverable by other users
- Shared: Only with specific people you choose

### Q: Can I delete my account and data?
**A:** Yes! You can delete your account at any time from Settings. This permanently removes all your data after a 30-day grace period.

### Q: Is DMLog COPPA compliant?
**A:** Yes! We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). Users under 13 require parental consent, and we don't collect personal information from children.

### Q: How do you handle payment information?
**A:** We use secure payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) and don't store credit card information on our servers. All payment processing follows PCI DSS standards.

### Q: Can I export my data?
**A:** Yes! You can export:
- Character sheets (PDF, JSON)
- Campaign data and notes
- Session transcripts
- Analytics and statistics
- Full account data package

### Q: What is your privacy policy?
**A:** Our full privacy policy is available at [dmlog.com/privacy](https://dmlog.com/privacy). It covers data collection, usage, sharing, and your rights as a user.
