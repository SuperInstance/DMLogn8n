## Production Guidelines

### Video Format Standards

**Technical Specifications:**
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Frame Rate: 30fps
- Audio: 48kHz, stereo, -6dB to -3dB peak
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Length: 2-5 minutes per video
- File size: Under 500MB per video

**Visual Style:**
- Clean, modern interface
- Consistent color scheme (DMLog brand colors)
- Clear cursor movement and highlighting
- Subtle animations and transitions
- Professional overlays and text

### Recording Equipment

**Screen Recording:**
- OBS Studio (free) or Camtasia (paid)
- 1920x1080 capture resolution
- Include system audio for notifications
- Highlight cursor movement
- Zoom in on important details

**Audio Recording:**
- USB microphone (Blue Yeti, Rode NT-USB)
- Pop filter for clean audio
- Quiet recording environment
- Script teleprompter app
- Audio editing in Audacity or Adobe Audition

### Editing Process

**Pre-Production:**
1. Review and finalize scripts
2. Prepare DMLog account with sample data
3. Test all features being demonstrated
4. Create checklist of recording shots

**Production:**
1. Record screen capture with voiceover
2. Record separate audio if needed
3. Capture multiple takes for complex sections
4. Record B-roll footage (app switching, loading screens)

**Post-Production:**
1. Sync audio and video
2. Add intro/outro music and branding
3. Insert text overlays and highlights
4. Add cursor effects and zooms
5. Color correction and audio cleanup
6. Export in multiple formats

### Distribution Strategy

**Platform Optimization:**
- YouTube: SEO-optimized titles, descriptions, tags
- Website: Embedded player with custom styling
- Social Media: Short clips and highlights
- Email Newsletter: Links to full series

**Engagement Features:**
- Timestamps in descriptions
- Related video suggestions
- Downloadable resources (cheat sheets)
- Community discussion prompts
- Feedback and comment monitoring

This comprehensive video tutorial series will help users of all skill levels master DMLog's features and enhance their D&D experience. The scripts provide detailed guidance while allowing for natural delivery and personalization.
